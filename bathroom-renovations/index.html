Bathroom projects

Planning your bathroom remodel

A bathroom has to work hard in a small space. Whether you want a walk-in shower, a new vanity or a full remodel, we begin with a site visit and a written quote so you know the scope of the job before work starts.

Bathroom projects usually begin with tear-out to the studs, so any issues behind the walls can be dealt with first. From there we handle waterproofing and tile, the vanity, mirror and fixtures, a ventilation fan to help control moisture, and paint and finishing.

Good waterproofing and ventilation matter most in a bathroom, especially through Thunder Bay winters when windows stay closed and moisture lingers.

What a bathroom project can include

  • Tear-out to the studs
  • Waterproofing and tile
  • Walk-in showers and tubs
  • Vanity, mirror and fixtures
  • Ventilation fan
  • Paint and finishing
